When a kitten moves in, everything is new: for the cat and for you. These five ideas can make the start easier. They do not replace veterinary advice; for any health questions, please always contact your vet.

## 1. A quiet place to retreat

Give your kitten a spot where it can withdraw, and let it discover your home step by step.

## 2. Regular routines

Regular times for eating, playing and resting give a sense of security. Cats value reliability.

## 3. Play and enrichment

Kittens have lots of energy. Variety in play and new little challenges keep everyday life exciting.

## 4. Important documents in one place

Vaccinations, appointments and notes from vet visits are worth a lot later if you keep them together in one place.

## 5. Observe and capture

Write down what you notice: new preferences, funny quirks, first times. In the first year, a lot changes quickly, and what you write down stays with you.
