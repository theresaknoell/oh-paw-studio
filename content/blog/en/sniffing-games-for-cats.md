Cats explore the world not only with their eyes but also with their noses. If you want to keep an indoor cat busy, you can speak to that sense directly. Sniffing games are quiet, need very little material and fit easily into everyday life.

## Why sniffing is good

Searching and discovering bring curiosity and hunting instinct into play without things getting wild. It is a lovely change of pace, especially for calm cats or on rainy days.

## Three ideas to start with

- **Treat search:** Hide a few treats or dry food in easy-to-reach spots, for example behind a cushion or under a towel.
- **Towel roll:** Loosely roll up a towel with small pieces of food in between. Your cat can unroll it herself.
- **Cardboard corner:** A box with a few holes and a bit of food inside invites exploring.

## How to make it work

Start simply and make the task only hard enough that your cat succeeds. Watch what she enjoys and build up slowly. Make sure nothing dangerous can be swallowed, and only offer food your cat already gets.

If you notice anything unusual about your cat's appetite or behaviour, please talk to your vet.
