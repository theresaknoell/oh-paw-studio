A kitten brings energy into every home. It explores, learns, sleeps a lot and grows faster than you might expect. In the first year especially, almost every week something changes, and you forget much of it faster than you think.

Every kitten develops at its own pace. This article does not set development norms but shows how you can record your observations. Please clarify questions about development and health with your vet.

## What you can record

- **Growth:** Weight and size, a photo from the same angle
- **New things:** Firsts, such as the first toy or the first jump onto the sofa
- **Preferences:** Food, favourite spots, games
- **Behaviour:** New quirks and habits
- **Appointments:** Vaccination and vet appointments with dates

## What the first year can look like

Instead of counting in weeks, rough stages can give you something to go by:

- **Settling in:** The first days and weeks in the new home. Everything is new, and the first routines form.
- **Discovering:** Your kitten gets bolder, plays more and tries things out.
- **Growing:** Daily life settles and you really get to know your kitten's quirks.
- **Maturing:** The kitten gradually becomes a young cat with firm preferences and habits.

How fast this happens differs from kitten to kitten. What matters is not what is "normal" but that you record what you experience.

## Start simple

A regular time each week, for example Sunday, is enough: a quick weigh-in, a photo, three lines of notes. Over time, a timeline emerges that you will love to look back on.

## Photos you can compare

Take the photo in the same place every week, for example on the same blanket, and place a familiar object next to your kitten, such as a toy. That way you can see later how much bigger she has grown. Short videos of a few seconds are worth it too, because movements and sounds change quickly.
