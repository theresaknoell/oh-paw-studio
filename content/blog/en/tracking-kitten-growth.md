A kitten brings energy into every home. It explores, learns, sleeps a lot and grows faster than you might expect. In the first year especially, almost every week something changes, and you forget much of it faster than you think.

Every kitten develops at its own pace. This article does not set development norms but shows how you can record your observations. Please clarify questions about development and health with your vet.

## What you can record

- **Growth:** Weight and size, a photo from the same angle
- **New things:** Firsts, such as the first toy or the first jump onto the sofa
- **Preferences:** Food, favourite spots, games
- **Behaviour:** New quirks and habits
- **Appointments:** Vaccination and vet appointments with dates

## Start simple

A regular time each week, for example Sunday, is enough: a quick weigh-in, a photo, three lines of notes. Over time, a timeline emerges that you will love to look back on.
