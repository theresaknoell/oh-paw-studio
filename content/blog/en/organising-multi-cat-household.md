Life with several cats means twice the activity: different personalities, individual preferences, different food and a shared routine that needs good organisation.

## A profile for every cat

Write down the key details for each cat: food, quirks, vet contact. That way you have everything at hand without having to think.

## Keep resources in view

It is often recommended to have at least one more litter box than the number of cats, plus several feeding spots and retreats. Check whether all your cats have enough room.

## Who eats what?

A simple overview with food amounts and special notes prevents mix-ups.

## Welcoming a new cat

Give everyone time and go step by step. Watch how your cats get along. You'll find helpful templates in the [Everyday care world](/en/products#care).

For questions about behaviour or health, your vet can help.
