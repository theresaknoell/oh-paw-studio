It all began with a book I hadn't even bought myself: a sponsored memory book for kittens. While filling it in, I noticed that many of the questions didn't fit our experiences, or the way I wanted to capture life with my cat.

The first year with a cat is something truly special. It is full of firsts, small discoveries and moments you can hardly piece together later if you haven't written them down.

## What mattered to me

A memory book should be **personal** and **thoughtful**. It should leave room for what really happens in your home, rather than only asking questions that are the same for everyone.

That is why I came up with a book that can be filled in digitally and still printed at home whenever you like.

## And what came next

From that one idea, more grew: digital products for everyday life with cats that are both useful and beautiful. That is how Oh Paw Studio came to be.
