A blank page can feel intimidating. With a few good questions, getting started is easier, and small answers gradually become a personal picture of your cat. You don't have to answer everything at once; a few lines are enough.

## Getting to know each other

- When and where did you first see each other?
- What was your first impression, and how did your cat behave?
- How did her name come about?

## The quirks

- What is her favourite spot, and at what time of day does she lie there?
- What sounds does she make when she wants something?
- What makes her purr every single time?

## The little rituals

- What does your morning routine look like?
- Is there a game only the two of you play?
- How does she greet you when you come home?

## The special moments

- What was the funniest thing that happened in recent weeks?
- What surprised you about her?
- What would you like to still know in ten years?

## Write it down, collect it, keep it

Create a regular place for your answers, such as a notebook or a file, and add to it whenever something comes to mind. A memory book can offer a lovely structure for this, with room for photos and thoughts. You'll find more in the [Memories world](/en/products?world=memories).
