At the vet, details matter: When was the last vaccine? Which medication helped? How long has a problem been going on? If you keep everything in one place, you are well prepared.

## What belongs in a health folder

- Cat profile and contact of your vet clinic
- Vaccinations and parasite prevention with dates
- Weight over time
- Treatments, medications and test results
- Expenses, if you want to keep an eye on them

## Before the appointment

Write down what you noticed, and collect your questions beforehand. After the appointment, note the key points straight away.

## Digital or on paper

Both work. What matters is having a fixed place. You'll find suitable templates in the [Everyday care world](/en/products?world=care).

These tips do not replace veterinary advice.
