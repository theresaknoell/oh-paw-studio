A kitten moves in, and suddenly everything is full of questions. A simple list helps you keep track. It does not replace veterinary advice; for any health questions, please contact your vet.

## Before the move-in

- Prepare food and water bowls, a litter box and litter
- Set up a quiet place to retreat
- Make your home safe: check cables, plants, open windows and small objects
- Get a scratching option and a first toy
- Plan a vet appointment and have the kitten's documents ready

## The first days

Give the kitten time to settle in. Show it food, water and the litter box, and watch how it moves and eats. A small log of the first seven days helps you retrace its development later.
