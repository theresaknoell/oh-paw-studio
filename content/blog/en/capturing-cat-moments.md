---
title: Capturing small cat moments – five ideas
date: 2026-11-12
excerpt: How to notice and keep the small things in everyday life with your cat.
---

It isn't only the big milestones that make life with a cat special. Often it is the small things you'll remember most fondly. These ideas help you capture them.

## 1. An evening note

Write down one sentence a day about what you noticed with your cat. After a few weeks, it becomes a personal diary.

## 2. Collect the quirks

Every cat has its habits: the favourite spot, the particular meow, the way it greets you. Capture them while they still feel ordinary to you.

## 3. Mark the firsts

The first day in a new home, the first toy, the first vet visit: such moments are forgotten faster than you think.

## 4. Don't forget ordinary days

Even a completely normal Sunday on the sofa deserves a place in your memories. Those are often the ones that feel most familiar later.

## 5. Have a regular place for it

Whether in a notebook or digitally: having a regular place for your memories makes it easier to keep going. A memory book can be a good start.
