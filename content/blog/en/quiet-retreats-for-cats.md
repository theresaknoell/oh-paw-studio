A cat-friendly home doesn't have to be chaotic. With a little planning, you can create a space where your cat feels safe that still looks like you.

## Quiet places

Cats look for places to rest that are sheltered and away from the busiest spots. A cave, a shelf or a cosy spot by the window can be enough.

## Lookout spots

Many cats like to watch from above. A raised spot with a view outside offers variety and an overview.

## Think room by room

Go through each room in your mind: Where is it quiet? Where could something become dangerous? Where does a cat spot fit in stylishly?
