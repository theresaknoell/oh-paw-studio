Cats are curious explorers with strong instincts. They want to hunt, sniff, solve puzzles and explore. In everyday indoor life, that often falls short. Even small changes bring variety.

## Play like a hunt

Move a toy like prey: hide it briefly, move it in jerks, and let your cat catch it in the end. Repeat this in short sessions that suit your cat.

## Sniffing and searching

Hide a few treats in cardboard boxes, towels or at different heights. It's fun and challenges the nose.

## Food as a task

Instead of simply filling the bowl, you can offer part of the food in a food puzzle. That turns a meal into a small task.

## Notice what your cat enjoys

Every cat has its own preferences. Note which games your cat likes best and repeat them. You'll find more inspiration in our [Home & enrichment world](/en/products#home).
